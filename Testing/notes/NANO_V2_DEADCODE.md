declaration of a `Proof/Tests` module (the owner keeps the non-vacuity tests). Layout as of the cleanup (4d17b0a).

**Method:** a constant is reachable if the type or value of a reachable constant uses it, transitively, starting
from the roots. Constructors of a reachable inductive also count.

**What is counted:** user declarations only. Auxiliary names are skipped: `match_`, `proof_`, `eq_`, `_`-internal
names, recursors, `noConfusion`, `inj`, `sizeOf` and instances.

**Result:** 39729 constants are reachable in total.
In Nano.V2Self modules: 9839 of 10173 counted declarations are reachable.

**Caveats:**
- Tactic-only modules (`Cert.Tac`) define tactics, not constants, so they show as dead although the certificate needs them to build.
- `#guard`, `example` and non-vacuity files (`OwnNV`) are tests, not dead code.
- A DECL-DEAD lemma may still be used by another dead lemma.
