## Workaround used: split big modules at top-level `def` boundaries (scratch only)

A module `M` is cut into `M0 .. M(k-1), M`: each part repeats the header (`set_option`s, `open`s, `namespace`s),
part `i` imports part `i-1`, the last part keeps the name `M`, so downstream imports are unchanged. No definition is
edited or reordered. `V8Base` in 3 parts: peak 8.0 GB instead of more than 13.9 GB. This is not in `regen.sh`; if
adopted it belongs in the generator (Sail has the module list) or in a post-processing step that has to be listed
in `DIFFS.md`. `DecodeEnd` can only be cut coarsely: 3 definitions are 10.5, 9.5 and 7.7 MB each
(`__DecodeA64_SME`, `__DecodeA64_DataProcFPSIMD`, `__DecodeT32`); `SysregsAutogen` has one of 6.5 MB.

## Effort estimate

* Model on 4.35.0: no source or generator change needed for elaboration as far as seen (32/34). What is needed is
  memory: either a box with clearly more than 14 GB (how much is unknown: unsplit `V8Base` exceeded 13.9 GB), or
  the module split (a script of about 20 lines, plus the `DIFFS.md` entry and a decision where it lives). Half a day
  including one full clean build, on a box with 32 GB or with the split.
* Proofs: unknown until the model is built. On 4.34.1 they take about 1000 s (`Words` 775 s). Budget one run
  (about 20 min) to get the per-file list, then fixes. `Words.lean` is generated, so a failure pattern there is
  fixed once in `gen/gen_words.py`.
* Total to get a definite answer: one session of about 3 h on a 32 GB box, or about 4 h here with the split
  (model about 1.5 h, proofs 20-30 min, margin for `DecodeEnd`).

## What to do differently next time

* Run one heavy `lean` at a time in this box; two together hit the shared 13.9 GB limit and both get killed.
* Start with the split for `V8Base`; the two unsplit attempts cost 42 min.
* A prebuilt-model cache per toolchain (as for 4.34.1) removes most of the cost.
